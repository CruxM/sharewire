Refund policy

Sharewire for Android, by Wire Studio · Updated 20 November 2026

Sharewire is free to use. Only Sharewire Premium is paid: a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase, all through Google Play. Premium removes the free transfer speed cap.

Cancelling a subscription

Cancel any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Sharewire Premium. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.

In the first 48 hours

Google Play handles refund requests made within 48 hours of buying. Open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & order history, choose Sharewire Premium and ask for a refund, or use Google Play's refund page.

After 48 hours

Email rmayank165@gmail.com within 30 days of buying, with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.). We refund Premium if a Premium feature doesn't work as described on your device and we can't fix it.

Refunds go back through Google Play to the way you paid. Once refunded, Premium is removed from that Google Play account.

Your rights

This policy doesn't take away any rights you have under consumer law where you live.
