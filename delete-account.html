SharewirePrivacy policy

Delete your Sharewire account and data

Sharewire for Android, by Mayank Kholiya

Your Sharewire account exists only on your device. We don't keep a copy on any server, so deleting it from the device deletes it completely.

In the app
  1. Open Sharewire and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Sign out. This deletes your account data from the device.
Delete everything

Uninstall Sharewire, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Sharewire → Storage → Clear storage. This also removes your transfer history.

What gets deleted

  • Your name and Google account ID: deleted when you sign out.
  • Birth year, transfer history and device settings: deleted when you uninstall or clear storage.

What is kept

  • Nothing is kept by us, because we never receive it.
  • Files you received stay in your Pictures, Movies and Download folders until you delete them yourself.
  • Your Google account itself isn't affected. To remove Sharewire's sign-in connection, visit myaccount.google.com/connections.
