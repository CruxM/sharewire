Privacy policy

Sharewire for Android · Effective 18 November 2026

Sharewire ("the app") is made by Mayank Kholiya ("we"). It sends files directly from your device to other devices near you on the same Wi-Fi network. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • We don't run servers for Sharewire, and nothing about you or your files is sent to us.
  • Your account details stay on your phone. Files go straight from your device to the device you choose, encrypted on the way.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Sharewire does not store your email address or profile photo. App data is excluded from Android backups.

Signing in with Google

Sign-in uses Google's Credential Manager. Google confirms your identity and gives the app your name and account ID. Google handles that sign-in under the Google Privacy Policy. We never see your Google password.

What other devices can see

While Sharewire is open and you are visible, devices running Sharewire on the same Wi-Fi network can see your device name and type (for example "Alex's phone", Android phone). You can rename your device or switch to Hidden at any time in the app.

Files you send and receive

Files are sent only when you choose to send them, and only to the device you pick. The receiver accepts each transfer (unless they turned on automatic accept for their own devices). Transfers go directly between the two devices over your local network, encrypted with TLS. They never pass through our servers or any cloud storage. Received photos and videos are saved to Pictures/Sharewire and Movies/Sharewire, other files to Download/Sharewire.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices / local network: to find and connect to other devices on your Wi-Fi. Sharewire can't work without it.
  • Network and Wi-Fi state, multicast: to know when you're on Wi-Fi and to discover devices.
  • Bluetooth (scan, advertise, connect): to find nearby devices and send files when there's no shared Wi-Fi.
  • Camera: only to scan another device's pairing code. Images are processed on your device and never saved or sent anywhere.

Sharing with third parties

We don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone. The app contains no advertising or analytics software.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything stays on your device until you remove it. Signing out in Settings deletes your name and Google account ID from the device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes all of its data, including your birth year and history. Files you received stay in your folders until you delete them. See how to delete your account and data.

Children

Sharewire is not directed at children under 13.

Security

Each device has its own keys kept in Android Keystore, and every transfer is encrypted. Because your data never leaves your devices except to the device you choose, there is no central copy that could leak.
